NBA Tier 1 vs. Tier 2 Institutes

Feature NBA Tier 1 Institutes NBA Tier 2 Institutes
Institutions Covered IITs, NITs, IIITs, Autonomous Institutes, Deemed Universities Affiliated Colleges under State/Private Universities
Curriculum Control Can design their own curriculum Follows university-prescribed syllabus
Washington Accord Recognition ✅ Yes (International Recognition) ❌ No (Only National Recognition)
Accreditation Scope Focused on Outcome-Based Education (OBE) for global standards Focused on meeting Indian education standards
Job & Higher Studies Benefits Better global job & higher education opportunities Limited to national job & education opportunities
Flexibility in Examination Autonomous exams and evaluation University-controlled exams
